Constellate

A Chronicle of Trade & War

You begin with one town on the grain lands. The land itself decides what a settlement can export — spend prosperity to found outposts and charter them as towns, and each will produce whatever its ground specializes in. Most goods need another good imported first before they can be exported at all.

Trade roads are just how goods move — the real game is choosing where to settle, what to build, and how to survive it. Every settlement has limits: overload one with routes and merchants abandon it, or worse. Each day brings its own event — harvests, blight, growth, war — and the crown will sometimes demand an answer of you. Watch your Emperor's Approval and the realm's War Risk, and raise walls where you can't afford to lose a town.
